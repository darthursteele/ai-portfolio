# Stage 3: Resume Gap Analysis

## Purpose

Systematically compare the candidate's resume against the JD. Identify what's strong, what's weak, and what's missing. This feeds the Stage 4 Q&A and informs Stage 5 strategy. Do not ask questions here — just analyze.

## The Business Problem Frame

Before the table, write a 2–3 sentence answer to: *What problem is the hiring manager trying to solve by filling this role?* Summarize the key business impact areas, deliverables, and what "great" looks like. This framing shapes everything downstream.

## Alignment Table

Create a comparison table:

| JD Requirement | Candidate's Evidence | Keyword Match (JD → Resume) | Alignment |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Top 7–10 critical requirements, ordered by importance] | [Specific bullets or roles from resume] | Present / Partial / Missing | Outstanding / Strong / Moderate / Weak / Missing |

Pull requirements from the JD's responsibilities AND qualifications sections. Don't just hit the bullet points — read for implicit requirements too (domain knowledge, pace expectations, tool fluency).

## Gap List

After the table, produce two lists:

**Hard gaps** — qualifications or experience the JD requires (or strongly implies) that the candidate clearly lacks. These need Stage 4 questions.

**Soft gaps** — areas where the candidate has relevant experience but the resume doesn't express it in the right language, with the right emphasis, or with enough evidence. These may be addressable without new information — or may reveal hidden depth in Stage 4.

## ATS Keyword Priorities

List the exact keywords and phrases from the JD to incorporate into the resume, prioritized by:
1. Frequency in the JD (how many times it appears)
2. Specificity (a precise technical term beats a generic one)
3. Likely ATS weight (title-adjacent terms, required skills)

Split into: must-include, should-include, nice-to-include.

## What Not to Do

- Do not write any resume copy here
- Do not ask gap questions here — that's Stage 4
- Do not assess overall competitiveness here — that's Stage 5
- Do not fabricate evidence of qualifications not present in the resume
