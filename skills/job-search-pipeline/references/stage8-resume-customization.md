# Stage 8: Full Resume Customization

## Inputs

By this point, the following are confirmed and locked:
- Approved positioning strategy (Stage 5)
- Approved headline (Stage 6)
- Approved professional summary (Stage 7)
- Gap-filling answers from Stage 4
- ATS keyword priorities from Stage 3

Do not revisit these. Apply them.

## Skills Section

- Update category headers to reflect JD priorities (e.g., if the JD emphasizes AI tools, the first skill category should name them explicitly)
- List only skills the candidate actually has — cross-reference Stage 4 answers before adding anything new
- Order categories by relevance to this specific role
- Within each category, front-load the highest-signal keywords

## Experience Section

For each role, in reverse chronological order:

**Context sentence (if not already present):** One sentence explaining the business problem this role was hired to solve and the scale of the environment. This grounds the bullets for a reader who doesn't know the company.

**Bullet rewriting rules:**
- Lead with a strong, specific action verb
- Include the outcome wherever possible — not just what was done, but what changed as a result
- ≥80% of bullets must include a quantified result (%, $, time saved, scale, adoption rate, headcount, revenue impact)
- Use JD terminology where it fits naturally — don't force it, but don't avoid it
- For older roles: compress. The farther back, the fewer bullets needed.
- No fabrication. No embellishment. If a number isn't in the resume or Stage 4 answers, don't invent one.

**What to surface from Stage 4:**
- Any achievements the user mentioned that weren't in the original resume → add as bullets
- Any context that reframes existing bullets → update the bullet or add context
- Any tools or technologies named that weren't in the original → add to skills or bullets where accurate

## Output

Save the complete resume as `{company-slug}_resume-optimized.md`.

Present it to the user with a short summary of the major changes made (not a list of every word changed — a summary of the strategic choices).

Ask: *"How does this look? Any bullets that don't sound like you, or anything you want to add, cut, or reframe?"*

Revise until the user explicitly approves. Do not move to Stage 9 without that approval.

## Critical Rules

- Write in first person throughout
- Avoid overused resume language: "spearheaded," "leveraged," "synergized," "utilized"
- The resume should read like a person wrote it, not like it was optimized
- Maintain complete truthfulness — the candidate will be asked about every bullet in an interview
- The approved headline and summary are locked — do not change them here
