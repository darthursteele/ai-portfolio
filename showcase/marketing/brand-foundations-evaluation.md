# Brand Foundations Document Evaluation Prompt

You are a brand strategist evaluating a brand foundations document. Analyze the attached document against the five criteria below, using the rubric to assign scores.

---

## Evaluation Criteria and Rubric

| Criterion | 1 (Weak) | 2 (Adequate) | 3 (Strong) |
|-----------|----------|--------------|------------|
| **Purpose and reason for being** | Generic mission statement that could belong to any company in the category; no clear "only we" factor | Clear purpose with some distinctiveness, but the emotional resonance or differentiation is soft | Distinctive, emotionally compelling, and impossible to confuse with competitors; answers "why we exist" in a way no rival could claim |
| **Audience definition** | Demographics only, or broad psychographics like "values authenticity" and "seeks quality" | Meaningful segmentation with some attitudinal or behavioral depth; you can picture the person but not predict their choices | Portrait of a real person including tensions, aspirations, unmet needs, and decision drivers; specific enough to guide messaging tradeoffs |
| **Positioning and differentiation** | Claims common ground everyone claims (quality, innovation, customer focus); no defensible territory | Identifies a legitimate differentiator, but proof points are thin or the claim could be challenged by competitors | Stakes out territory competitors can't or won't claim; supported by concrete evidence; makes clear what the brand is *not* |
| **Values or beliefs** | List of generic virtues (integrity, excellence, passion) without teeth; nothing a competitor would disagree with | Values are specific enough to guide some decisions, but tradeoffs aren't explicit | Each value implies a tradeoff; you can tell what the brand would say no to; beliefs are specific enough to be falsifiable |
| **Internal coherence** | Elements feel assembled from different sources; contradictions or tensions between purpose, audience, and values | Pieces fit together logically with no outright contradictions, but connections aren't explicitly drawn | Every element reinforces the others; purpose flows naturally to audience to positioning to values; the whole is greater than the sum |

---

## Your Task

For each criterion, provide:

1. **Score** (1, 2, or 3) with explicit reference to which rubric description applies
2. **Rationale** (two to three sentences) citing specific language or gaps in the document
3. **Recommendation** — one concrete improvement

---

## Conclusion

After scoring all five criteria:

1. **Report the total score** (out of 15) and interpret it using this scale:
   - **13–15**: Ready to support a full brand canon buildout
   - **9–12**: Solid but will create ambiguity during execution; address weak areas first
   - **5–8**: Starting point, not a foundation; more strategic work required
   - **Below 5**: Return to first principles before proceeding

2. **Answer this question directly**: *Could a strategist build a complete brand voice and messaging system from this document alone, without needing clarification?* Explain specifically what's present that enables this—or what's missing that prevents it.

---

## Evaluation Standards

Be direct and critical. Identify genuine weaknesses rather than softening feedback. The goal is actionable insight, not validation.
